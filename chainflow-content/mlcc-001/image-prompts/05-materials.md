# 05｜材料壁垒 — 视觉锚点提示词

## image2-primary

```
Scanning electron microscope (SEM) aesthetic image showing barium titanate ceramic powder particles at nanoscale. Spherical and near-spherical particles ranging from 100-300nm in diameter, densely packed but individually distinguishable. High contrast black and white with subtle warm tone in the highlights. The image should feel like genuine scientific research documentation — slightly grainy, high magnification, authentic laboratory aesthetic. Dark background. Suitable for use as a full-page background texture or section image. No text, no scale bars, no labels, no annotations. 3:4 vertical.
```

画面主体：BaTiO3 陶瓷粉体纳米颗粒的 SEM 风格图像
构图：均匀分布，满版
视角：俯视（SEM 标准）
风格：科学文献中的 SEM 图像质感——真实的实验室美学
色彩：黑白高对比 + 微弱暖色在高光处
材质：纳米颗粒的表面纹理
光线：SEM 固有的电子束"照明"质感
用于页面区域：材料栈背景或局部纹理
禁止元素：标尺、文字、标注
