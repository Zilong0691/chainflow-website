# 10｜ChainFlow 判断 — 视觉锚点提示词

## image2-primary

```
A calm, expansive waterscape at dawn — a wide river or lake with subtle ripples catching the first light. The water is dark and substantial, not bright and cheerful. In the distance, faint outlines of industrial infrastructure — ports, cranes, warehouses — barely visible through morning mist. The metaphor: supply chains flowing like water, infrastructure fading into the background, the focus is on flow itself. Cinematic, meditative, but grounded. Dark navy and steel grey tones with a single warm accent on the horizon. 3:4 vertical. No text. Use as full-page background.
```

画面主体：黎明时分的开阔水面——远处隐约可见工业基础设施（港口/仓库/起重机）的轮廓
隐喻："让供应链如水一般"——水流本身是主角，基础设施隐在背景
构图：水面占据画面主体，地平线在下 1/3 处
风格：电影的、沉静的、但接地气的
色彩：深海军蓝 + 钢灰，地平线上一抹暖色
光线：黎明第一道光
用于页面区域：全页背景（降低不透明度或直接作为氛围底图）
禁止元素：文字、显著的建筑物品牌
