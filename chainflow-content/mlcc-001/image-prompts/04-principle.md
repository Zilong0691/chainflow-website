# 04｜工作原理与参数选型 — 视觉锚点提示词

## image2-primary

```
Abstract macro photography of a circuit board surface in extreme close-up, focused on a cluster of tiny MLCC capacitors (0402 and 0603 sizes) densely packed near a large BGA chip. Shallow depth of field — the capacitors in the foreground are razor sharp, the chip and board gradually blur into the background. Warm PCB traces visible beneath the components. Dark, moody lighting with a single overhead source creating subtle reflections on the metal end caps. The image communicates the density and scale of passive components on modern electronics. Industrial archaeology aesthetic. No text, no labels. 3:4 vertical format. Use as full-width background or section divider image.
```

画面主体：PCB 上密集排列的 MLCC 集群，背景有一颗大的 BGA 芯片
构图：MLCC 在前景清晰，芯片在中景虚化
视角：极端微距，约 5-10× 放大
风格：工业考古学美学——电路板像古代遗迹一样被照亮
色彩：暗调，PCB 的深绿/黑 + 焊盘的金 + MLCC 的暖陶色
材质：焊点、铜箔、陶瓷体、芯片封装的多种材质对比
光线：单一顶光源，戏剧性的明暗
留白位置：画面上方和边侧留暗区
用于页面区域：全页背景或页面上半部
禁止元素：可辨识的芯片品牌、文字、丝印
