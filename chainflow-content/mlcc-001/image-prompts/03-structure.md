# 03｜技术结构 — 视觉锚点提示词

## image2-primary

```
Scientific cross-section SEM-style image of an MLCC multilayer ceramic capacitor, perfectly cut through the vertical center. The internal structure reveals alternating layers of ceramic dielectric (lighter warm beige-grey bands) and nickel internal electrodes (darker metallic grey bands). Approximately 15-30 visible layer pairs. The odd-numbered electrode layers clearly extend to the left termination, even-numbered layers to the right termination — this alternating connection pattern must be visible. Left and right termination electrodes show three distinct layers: copper base (warm brown), nickel barrier (grey), and tin plating (light silver). Scientific imaging aesthetic — like a Nature or Science journal cover. Dark background. High contrast material definition between ceramic and metal. Monochromatic with subtle warmth in the ceramic layers. No text, no scale bars, no labels, no annotations. Vertical 3:4 format suitable for half-page layout.
```

画面主体：MLCC 正中纵向剖面的 SEM 风格图像
构图：剖面居中，占画面主体
视角：正视剖面
风格：Nature/Science 期刊封面级科学成像
色彩：灰阶为主，陶瓷层略偏暖
材质：陶瓷层与金属层的高对比度材质差异
光线：SEM 风格的均匀"照明"，材质本身产生对比
留白位置：画面左下右留边
透明背景：不需要（深色底）
推荐比例：1080×1440（可裁切用于半栏）
用于页面区域：左栏 55% 主图
禁止出现的元素：任何文字、标尺、箭头、标注、数据
不能生成的内容：不要生成"看起来像电路板"的剖面——这必须是 MLCC 器件剖面
生成后核验：奇偶层连接方向正确（奇数左/偶数右）· 端电极三层结构可见· 层数 > 10 层· 不是只有 3-4 层· 无乱码结构

High-precision 3D cutaway render of an MLCC capacitor. The front half is removed to reveal the internal alternating layers. Soft studio lighting from upper left creating gentle shadows that define the layered structure. Clean dark grey background. Technical illustration quality — accurate enough for an engineering presentation but beautiful enough for a magazine. No text, no labels. 3:4 vertical.
