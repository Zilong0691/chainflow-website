## image2-primary

```
Interior of an electronic component cleanroom manufacturing facility. Rows of precision tape-casting or screen-printing machinery under yellow cleanroom lighting. The atmosphere is sterile, precise, industrial. Shot from a slight elevation looking down the length of the production line. The repetition of machinery creates a rhythmic pattern. Subdued warm yellow-amber lighting characteristic of cleanrooms. No people visible or only distant silhouettes in bunny suits. This is where ceramic layers are born. Archival industrial photography — not promotional corporate glossy. No logos, no brand names, no text. Vertical 3:4. Dark and atmospheric.
```
