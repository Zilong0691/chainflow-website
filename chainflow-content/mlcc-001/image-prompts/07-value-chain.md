# 07｜产业格局 — 视觉锚点提示词

## image2-primary

```
Aerial or satellite-style photograph showing the geographical concentration of global electronics manufacturing — the East Asian coastline from Japan through Korea, Taiwan, to southern China. Dense urban-industrial clusters visible as glowing nodes connected by invisible supply lines. The image should feel like an intelligence briefing map — dark, atmospheric, with scattered points of warm light indicating manufacturing hubs. Not a literal satellite photo — more like a stylized night-earth image with enhanced industrial clusters. Dark navy and charcoal tones with warm amber/gold points of light. No text, no country labels, no borders. 3:4 vertical format. Use as full-page background at reduced opacity (15-25%).
```

画面主体：东亚电子制造带的夜景/航拍——日本→韩国→台湾→华南的工业聚集区光点
构图：东亚海岸线纵向分布
风格：情报简报地图的美学——不是精确卫星图，是强化工业聚集区的夜光图
色彩：深海军蓝/炭灰底色 + 暖琥珀/金色光点
光线：光点代表制造中心
用于页面区域：产业格局页全页背景底纹
禁止元素：国界线、地名、任何文字标注

## image2-alternative

```
A dimly lit electronics components warehouse or distribution center. Floor-to-ceiling shelves filled with component reels in anti-static bags. A solitary figure in the distance reviewing inventory. The scale of stored components is overwhelming — millions of tiny parts waiting to enter supply chains. Dark, slightly cool tone. Industrial realism. No visible logos. 3:4 vertical.
```

画面主体：电子元器件仓库——满墙的料盘，远处一个模糊的人影
风格：冷调工业现实主义——"供应链等待"的隐喻
