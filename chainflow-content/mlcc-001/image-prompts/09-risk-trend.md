# 09｜需求风险与替代决策 — 视觉锚点提示词

## image2-primary

```
Dramatic still life photograph of a single MLCC capacitor balanced precariously on the edge of a circuit board trace, like a tightrope walker. Below it — darkness. The visual metaphor is "one tiny component, one big risk". The capacitor is in sharp focus, the PCB trace leads off into blur. High contrast lighting. Dark, moody, cinematic. The image should evoke the fragility of supply chains — how one small missing part can halt everything. 3:4 vertical. No text.
```

画面主体：一颗 MLCC 像走钢丝的人一样立在一条电路板走线的边缘，下方是黑暗
隐喻：一颗小器件，整个系统悬在上面——供应链脆弱性
构图：器件居中，走线从左或右下延伸，下方大面积暗黑
风格：电影感静物摄影——戏剧性的紧张感
光线：高对比度单光源
用于页面区域：页面上半部（供需剪刀差区上方）或整页背景
禁止元素：文字、标注

## image2-alternative

```
An hourglass where the upper half contains a cascade of tiny MLCC capacitors instead of sand. The capacitors are flowing through the narrow neck — some stuck, creating a bottleneck. The lower half is nearly empty. The metaphor: supply is running out and the bottleneck is real. Dark background, dramatic lighting, the capacitors catching glints of warm light. Editorial conceptual photography. 3:4 vertical. No text.
```

画面主体：沙漏——上半个装着 MLCC 器件，在瓶颈堵住了，下半个几乎空的
隐喻：供应在流失，瓶颈真实存在
