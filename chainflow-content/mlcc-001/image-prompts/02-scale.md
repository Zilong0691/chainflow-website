# 02｜尺度冲击 — 视觉锚点提示词

## image2-primary

```
Comparison scale photography showing the dramatic size range of MLCC capacitors. In the center, a standard 0402 MLCC (1.0x0.5mm) photographed next to a single grain of rice and a sesame seed on a dark matte surface. The MLCC is in sharp focus, showing its warm beige ceramic body and silver metallic end caps. A human hair lies across the frame for additional scale reference. Clean, scientific presentation lighting. The image communicates "this is shockingly small" without words. Dark charcoal background. Editorial product photography style. 3:4 vertical format. No text, no labels, no scale bars.
```

画面主体：一粒米 + 一粒芝麻 + 一颗 MLCC + 一根头发丝 并排放在深色平面上
构图：水平排列，MLCC 居中。头发丝横贯画面
视角：微距平视，约 2-3× 放大
风格：科学展示摄影，干净的光线
色彩：深炭灰背景 + 米的白/芝麻的棕/MLCC 的暖陶瓷色/头发丝的深色
材质：有机物（米/芝麻）vs 工业制品（MLCC）的材质对比
光线：柔和的顶光，无强烈阴影
留白位置：上下方留暗区
透明背景：不需要
推荐比例：1080×1440
用于页面区域：页面上 60% 尺度对比区主图
禁止出现的元素：文字、标尺、刻度、任何标注
生成后核验：MLCC 形状准确 · 尺度对比真实（米粒~5mm，MLCC~1mm）· 无乱码

```
A dramatic macro photograph of an 0402 MLCC capacitor sitting on a fingertip. The capacitor is perfectly sharp, revealing ceramic texture and metallic end caps. The fingertip fills the lower portion of frame, providing human scale. The component appears tiny against the fingerprint ridges. Dark background with soft top lighting. This image immediately communicates miniaturization to any viewer. 3:4 vertical. Editorial, not commercial product shot.
```
